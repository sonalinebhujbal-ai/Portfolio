CASE STUDY 01
AML Compliance Release Governance — EverBank
Business Problem

AML compliance changes lacked clear requirement coverage and governance checkpoints, leading to defect leakage into production and inconsistent audit artefacts across workstreams.

My Role

Functional Delivery Lead acting as Business Analyst — responsible for requirements validation, gap analysis, UAT design, stakeholder coordination, and governance documentation.

Approach

Reviewed regulatory requirements against functional specs, identified coverage gaps, introduced governance checkpoints into the release pipeline, and built reusable traceability and documentation frameworks.

Analysis Performed

Requirements analysis · Gap analysis · UAT scenario design · Traceability mapping · Stakeholder alignment workshops · Risk identification and mitigation planning · Artifact consistency review.

Outcome

Delivered 20+ audit-compliant AML releases with zero critical production escapes. Requirement clarity improved by ~30% reduction in requirement-related defects. Governance frameworks adopted org-wide, improving artefact consistency by 15–20%.

Enterprise AML Monitoring Optimization — CIBC
Business Problem

Approach

Analysis Performed

Outcome

30% improvement in operational efficiency. 20% reduction in rework. 15% increase in on-time sprint completion over 12 months. Strengthened audit readiness across enterprise compliance releases. KPI dashboards adopted by senior leadership for data-driven risk assessment.

CASE STUDY 03
SEC Trade Surveillance Incident Governance — Morgan Stanley
Business Problem

Recurring production incidents on SEC-regulated Trade Surveillance platforms created regulatory risk and operational instability, with inadequate documentation for root cause prevention.

My Role

L3 Production Support and Functional Governance — managing Tier-3 incidents, performing root cause analysis, building runbooks, coordinating deployments, and producing operational reporting.

Approach

Structured RCA methodology for each recurring incident; developed preventive monitoring runbooks and incident playbooks; introduced change control governance for compliance application onboarding.

Analysis Performed

Root cause analysis · Incident trend analysis · Process documentation · Change control governance · Deployment coordination · Senior management reporting.

Outcome

Reduced recurring production incidents and improved platform reliability for SEC-regulated systems. Governance documentation became the standard onboarding resource for incoming team members. Incident trend reports enabled proactive leadership decision-making on platform risks.
